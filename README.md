# AIPINTAI · AI平台网

**AIPINTAI 是一个月阅读量 8–10 万的中文 AI 工具导航与资讯网站。** 网站已收录 **1,000+ 个 AI 工具**，及 **500+ 篇 AI 资讯**。我们免费为中文读者介绍全球优秀的 AI 工具，并分享行业动态。

如果你有 AI 产品、工具更新或行业资讯要发布，欢迎与我们联系。AIPINTAI 可以成为你触达中文读者的发布渠道伙伴。

想找一款能用在工作里的 AI 工具，或弄清一个新出现的 AI 概念？AIPINTAI 把工具入口、中文解读和行业动态放在一个地方，方便个人用户、开发者和小团队按需求查找。

[访问官网](https://www.aipintai.com/) · [提交 AI 工具](https://www.aipintai.com/?plugin=nciaer_form&formid=1) · [发布合作](https://www.aipintai.com/bd.html) · [反馈与建议](https://github.com/hogoai/aipintai.com/issues)

## English overview

**AIPINTAI** is a Chinese-language AI tools directory and knowledge site. It helps readers browse 16 categories spanning creative tools, productivity, industry applications, and AI development, and follow industry news.

AIPINTAI lists **1,000+ AI tools**, features **500+ AI news articles**, and receives **80,000–100,000 reads per month**. It introduces AI tools from around the world to Chinese-speaking readers for free. If you have an AI tool, product update, or industry news to share, [get in touch about publishing with us](https://www.aipintai.com/bd.html).

This repository is the project's public introduction and feedback hub. [Visit AIPINTAI](https://www.aipintai.com/) or [share feedback on GitHub](https://github.com/hogoai/aipintai.com/issues).
